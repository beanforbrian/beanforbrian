Selma Wilson — The Superhero Behind the StorySelma Wilson — A Super-Heroína por Trás da História

Selma & Herman Wilson

Brian & Mama SelmaBrian & Mamãe Selma

Selma, Herman & firstborn DarrylSelma, Herman & primogênito Darryl

Selma Wilson was not just a mother. She was the backbone, the heartbeat, and the quiet force that made everything possible. A phenomenal mother of 9 children and 16 grandchildren, she served as Herman's personal nurse through 17 years of home dialysis in Jackson, Mississippi — providing guidance, nourishment, and unwavering love every single day.Selma Wilson não era apenas uma mãe. Ela era a espinha dorsal, o coração e a força silenciosa que tornava tudo possível. Uma mãe fenomenal de 9 filhos e 16 netos, ela serviu como enfermeira pessoal de Herman durante 17 anos de diálise em casa em Jackson, Mississippi.

In 1997, when Brian gave his kidney to his father, Selma traveled tirelessly between Birmingham and Jackson — holding both her husband and son through surgery, recovery, and transition. She held the family together at its most fragile moment.Em 1997, quando Brian doou seu rim ao pai, Selma viajou incansavelmente entre Birmingham e Jackson — sustentando marido e filho durante a cirurgia, a recuperação e a transição.

She was the family's and community's superhero, griot, mother, friend, sister, healer, and all-purpose doer of it all.Ela era a super-heroína da família e da comunidade, griô, mãe, amiga, irmã, curadora e realizadora de tudo.

"Oh how I wish she were here to provide her magic in this stage. Thank you mama for all that you do, did, and the rich legacy you left. We love and miss you very much." — Brian Wilson"Oh, como eu gostaria que ela estivesse aqui para oferecer sua magia nesta fase. Obrigado, mamãe, pelo rico legado que deixou. Nós te amamos e sentimos muito a sua falta." — Brian Wilson

Brian's StoryA História de Brian

From the very beginning — a life shaped by love and sacrificeDesde o início — uma vida moldada pelo amor e sacrifício

Camp Wilson 917

Jackson, Mississippi — Early YearsJackson, Mississippi — Anos Iniciais
Brian Wilson was born into a family of nine children. His parents Herman and Selma raised their children by example — with sacrifice, generosity, and love as their native language.Brian Wilson nasceu em uma família de nove filhos. Seus pais Herman e Selma criaram seus filhos pelo exemplo — com sacrifício, generosidade e amor como língua nativa.
Age 10 — A Child's PromiseAos 10 anos — A Promessa de uma Criança
When Brian was just 10 years old, his father Herman was diagnosed with renal failure. Without hesitation, Brian told his parents: "If Daddy ever needs a kidney, he can have one of mine."Quando Brian tinha apenas 10 anos, seu pai Herman foi diagnosticado com insuficiência renal. Sem hesitar, Brian disse: "Se papai precisar de um rim, ele pode ter um dos meus."
1980–1997 — Seventeen Years of Dialysis1980–1997 — Dezessete Anos de Diálise
For 17 years, Herman endured dialysis administered at home by Selma. She was his nurse, his caretaker, his daily sustainer. Brian never forgot his promise.Por 17 anos, Herman passou por diálise administrada em casa por Selma. Brian nunca esqueceu sua promessa.
September 15, 1997 — The Gift15 de setembro de 1997 — O Dom
At 23 years old, Brian donated one of his kidneys to his father at UAB Hospital in Birmingham. The surgeon called it "one of the healthiest kidneys he had ever seen." Herman said: "That was just an outstanding sacrifice for a son to make for his father."Aos 23 anos, Brian doou um de seus rins ao pai no Hospital UAB em Birmingham. O cirurgião disse ser "um dos rins mais saudáveis que já havia visto."
The EducatorO Educador
Brian built a career as a deeply committed educator — making profound connections with every student, approaching each day as both teacher and lifelong learner.Brian construiu uma carreira como educador profundamente comprometido — criando conexões profundas com cada aluno.
Today — Nearly 29 Years LaterHoje — Quase 29 Anos Depois
Brian's remaining kidney is now failing. He is on the transplant waiting list at Piedmont Hospital, Atlanta and expects to be added at UAB Hospital, Birmingham.O rim restante de Brian está falhando. Ele está na lista de espera para transplante no Hospital Piedmont, Atlanta e espera ser adicionado no Hospital UAB, Birmingham.
